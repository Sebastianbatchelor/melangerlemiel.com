Biography

Mélanger Le Miel is the moniker of young Geneva-born musician and DJ Sebastian Batchelor. Area 3000 Radio resident, synth player in Flavour, and founder of artist platform Mélange, his sets are a positive, genre-roaming take on dance music shaped by a life between Switzerland and Australasia. Supported by Ben UFO, Courtesy, Dr Banana, Edd Fisher, Zuri, Kailyn Crabbe and more, who describe his music as ‘having an itchy groove’, he blends House, UKG, Techno and Breakbeats, marrying warm piano and live instruments with clubby, bass-heavy drum breaks. Seb has performed at some of dance music’s finest gatherings, from Sneaky Pete’s, The Gasometer, Venue M.O.T and Corsica Studios to Lost Village & Days Festival.
